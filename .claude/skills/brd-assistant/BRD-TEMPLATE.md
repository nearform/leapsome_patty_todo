# Business Requirements Document (BRD) - Creation Template

## Document Structure

Follow this structure and include these sections:

### 1. Executive Summary
- Brief overview of the strategic initiative
- Problem statement summary
- Proposed solution overview

### 2. Introduction

#### Project Background
- Context and history leading to this initiative
- Current state description
- Business drivers and motivations

#### Problem Statement
- Detailed problem description
- Impact of current situation
- Challenges to address
- Scope boundaries

#### Project Goals
- Primary objectives (numbered list)
- Expected outcomes
- Success criteria
- High-level timeline and milestones

### 3. User Analysis

#### User Personas
For each primary user type, include:
- **Name & Role**: Representative name and job title
- **Background**: Experience level, responsibilities, current tools/systems used
- **Goals**: What they're trying to achieve
- **Pain Points**: Current frustrations and challenges (bulleted list)
- **Needs**: Specific requirements to address their pain points (bulleted list)
- **Access Patterns**: How frequently they'll use the system, peak usage times, typical session duration

### 4. Strategic Risk and Assumption Analysis

#### SWOT Analysis
**Strengths:**
- Internal positive factors (bulleted list)

**Weaknesses:**
- Internal limitations or challenges (bulleted list)

**Opportunities:**
- External favourable conditions (bulleted list)

**Threats:**
- External risks or challenges (bulleted list)

#### Business Risk Register
For each identified business risk, provide:

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Impact | Probability | Mitigation Strategy | Owner |
|---------|-----------------|---------|-------------|-------------------|--------|

- **Risk ID**: Sequential identifier (R1, R2, etc.)
- **Risk Description**: Clear description of the risk
- **Impact**: High/Medium/Low
- **Probability**: High/Medium/Low
- **Mitigation Strategy**: Specific actions to reduce risk
- **Owner**: Role responsible for managing the risk

Include 8-12 key **business and strategic** risks covering:
- Market risks
- User adoption risks
- Budget and funding risks
- Organisational change risks
- Strategic alignment risks
- Regulatory and compliance risks
- Vendor/partner risks

**Note:** Technical and implementation risks will be covered in the Technical Architecture Document.

#### Business Assumptions
Numbered list of key assumptions underlying the project:
- Market assumptions
- User behaviour assumptions
- Organisational readiness assumptions
- Budget and resource availability assumptions
- Timeline assumptions
- Regulatory environment assumptions
- Stakeholder availability and engagement assumptions

Include 10-15 critical business assumptions.

**Note:** Technical assumptions will be defined in the Technical Architecture Document.

### 5. Document Scope

#### What This Document Covers
- Business context and strategic rationale
- User personas and their needs (single source of truth for all documents)
- Business goals and success criteria
- Strategic and business risks
- Business assumptions

#### What This Document Does NOT Cover
- Feature specifications → See PRD
- Technical requirements and constraints → See PRD and TAD
- Implementation details → See TAD
- Technical risks → See TAD
- Team structure and timelines → See Project Implementation Plan

---

## Key Principles
1. **User-Centred**: Ground all requirements in actual user needs and pain points
2. **Business-Focused**: Emphasise business value and strategic alignment
3. **Risk-Aware**: Proactively identify and plan for business and strategic risks
4. **Realistic**: Base assumptions on known constraints and realities
5. **Actionable**: Provide enough detail for stakeholders to make decisions
6. **Strategic**: Maintain focus on business outcomes, not technical solutions

---

The final BRD should integrate all these elements in a clear, cohesive format, suitable for both executive review and as a foundation for the PRD and Technical Architecture documents.
